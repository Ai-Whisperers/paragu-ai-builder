# Feature Gap Analysis — What 1st-World Competitors Have That Our Leads Don't

*Researched April 2026. Sources: Colorlib, Squarespace, Wix, Shopify, industry analysis.*

---

## Executive Summary

Across all 99 leads, our preview sites currently have **basic sections**: hero, services, team, gallery, contact, WhatsApp, FAQ. 

1st-world competitors (US, Europe, Australia) consistently include **12 additional features** our previews lack. Implementing these would significantly increase conversion rates.

## What We Should Build Next (Recommended Order)

1. **Gift cards module** — reusable across all lead types, generates direct revenue
2. **Before/after gallery component** — critical for depilacion, estetica, barberia
3. **Google Reviews live widget** — low effort, high trust impact
4. **Multi-location selector** — for TAJOS and other chains
5. **Product e-commerce for all types** — retail upsell
6. **Membership/subscription per business type** — recurring revenue

All these can be built as reusable section components in our engine and enabled per-tenant via `features` in `site.json`.
