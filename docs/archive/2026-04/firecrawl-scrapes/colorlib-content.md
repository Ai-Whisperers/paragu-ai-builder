**Looking for sushi restaurant website inspiration?**

Your sushi restaurant’s website should reflect the artistry and precision of Japanese cuisine — showcasing your menu, ambiance, and making reservations effortless.

We’ve curated 19 of the best sushi and Japanese restaurant websites — from intimate omakase counters to modern sushi bars.

Each entry includes the platform it’s built on, so you can roll out your own [restaurant website](https://colorlib.com/wp/restaurant-website-designs/).

## Best Sushi Restaurant Website Examples

### 1\. [Omakase (SF)](https://omakasesf.com/)

**Built with**: **[Squarespace](https://colorlib.com/go/squarespace)**

![omakase (sf) website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/omakasesf-com.jpg)

Intimate 12-seat Edomae-style sushi restaurant. Traditional Japanese preparation techniques including aging, sujime, and konba jime..

Minimalist single-page layout that evokes Tokyo intimacy, muted tones with high-quality close-up food photography.

**What stands out:** The clean, minimalist layout lets the work speak for itself — no distractions, just great design.

### 2\. [Hoseki Omakase Sushi Bar](https://hosekisushi.com/)

**Built with**: **[Squarespace](https://colorlib.com/go/squarespace)**

![hoseki omakase sushi bar website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/hosekisushi-com.jpg)

Family-owned Edomae-style sushi restaurant with Master Chef Tim (30+ years experience). Intimate omakase counter experience..

Warm, inviting Squarespace design showcasing chef craftsmanship and premium ingredients with weekly-updated sourcing info.

**What stands out:** Warm tones and inviting imagery make visitors feel welcome before they even walk through the door.

### 3\. [Omakase Yume](https://omakaseyume.com/)

**Built with**: **[Wix](https://colorlib.com/out/wixsmallbiz)**

![omakase yume website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/omakaseyume-com.jpg)

Chef’s omakase tasting of 16 courses, only 6 seats per seating at 5PM, 7PM, and 9PM. Ultra-intimate dining experience..

Wix-built site with dark, atmospheric design matching the exclusive 6-seat concept.

**What stands out:** The dark, atmospheric design creates a bold visual identity that perfectly suits Omakase Yume’s brand.

### 4\. [Sushi W](https://sushiwny.com/)

**Built with**: **[Wix](https://colorlib.com/out/wixsmallbiz)**

![sushi w website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/sushiwny-com.jpg)

Affordable omakase sushi bar founded in 2021. Three omakase tiers ($38-$68), no-tipping policy.

Each piece handcrafted in front of diners.. Modern Wix design focused on accessibility and clear pricing communication for the budget omakase concept.

**What stands out:** The modern, sleek design feels current and forward-thinking — exactly what clients look for.

### 5\. [Omakase Sushi & Sake Bar](https://omakaselex.com/)

**Built with**: **[Wix](https://colorlib.com/out/wixsmallbiz)**

![omakase sushi & sake bar website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/omakaselex-com.jpg)

Omakase-style sushi restaurant with premium sake and Japanese whisky bar selection..

Wix-built site with clean presentation of menus and reservation system.

**What stands out:** The clean, minimalist layout lets the work speak for itself — no distractions, just great design.

### 6\. [Omakase Japanese Sushi Bar](https://omakasearuba.com/)

**Built with**: **[Wix](https://colorlib.com/out/wixsmallbiz)**

![omakase japanese sushi bar website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/omakasearuba-com.jpg)

Fine dining Japanese sushi bar on the island of Aruba, located at Manchebo resort..

Tropical-meets-Japanese aesthetic on Wix with resort-style imagery and reservation functionality.

**What stands out:** Integrated booking functionality turns website visitors into booked appointments.

### 7\. [Masa](https://masanyc.com/)

**Built with**: **Webflow**

![masa website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/masanyc-com.jpg)

Legendary fine-dining omakase by Chef Masayoshi Takayama. One of the most expensive restaurants in America. Michelin-starred.

Philosophy of “shibui” (refined simplicity).. Understated Webflow design reflecting shibui philosophy, elegant restraint with minimal text and premium imagery.

**What stands out:** The clean, minimalist layout lets the work speak for itself — no distractions, just great design.

### 8\. [Bondi Sushi](https://bondisushi.com/)

**Built with**: **Webflow**

![bondi sushi website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/bondisushi-com.jpg)

Modern sushi bar offering nigiri, hand rolls, sashimi, crispy rice, combo boxes, and catering. Sister bar Shinji’s provides sake and cocktails..

Vibrant Webflow design with animated elements, bold typography, and lifestyle photography. Multi-location showcase.

**What stands out:** Bold typography and strong visual hierarchy create an immediately memorable first impression.

### 9\. [Omakase @ Barracks Row](https://omakasedc.com/)

**Built with**: **Webflow**

![omakase @ barracks row website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/omakasedc-com.jpg)

Sushi omakase counter honoring Japanese tradition while pushing flavor boundaries. Two nightly seatings only (5:30pm and 8:15pm), Resy reservations required..

GoDaddy/Webflow-built with dramatic dark design, emphasizing exclusivity and the intimate counter experience.

**What stands out:** The dark, atmospheric design creates a bold visual identity that perfectly suits Omakase @ Barracks Row’s brand.

### 10\. [Uchi Restaurants](https://uchirestaurants.com/)

**Built with**: **WordPress**

![uchi restaurants website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/uchirestaurants-com.jpg)

Non-traditional Japanese cuisine by James Beard Award-winning Chef Tyson Cole. Includes Uchi, Uchiko, Uchibā, and Oheya (12-seat omakase)..

Polished WordPress build with WooCommerce, showcasing multiple concepts and locations with strong editorial photography.

**What stands out:** Stunning photography elevates the entire site and makes the products look irresistible.

### 11\. [Kissaki](https://explorekissaki.com/)

**Built with**: **WordPress**

![kissaki website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/explorekissaki-com.jpg)

Contemporary Japanese restaurant with traditional cuisine and modern innovation. Executive Chef Edgar Valerio. Omakase counter, kaiseki, and at-home omakase experiences..

Feature-rich WordPress site with online ordering, reservations, and editorial content. Press mentions prominently displayed.

**What stands out:** The modern, sleek design feels current and forward-thinking — exactly what clients look for.

### 12\. [KazuNori](https://handrollbar.com/)

**Built with**: **WordPress**

![kazunori website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/handrollbar-com.jpg)

“The Original Hand Roll Bar” by Sushi Nozawa Group. Fresh hand rolls with crispy nori, warm rice, and premium ingredients..

Warm, inviting WordPress design with location-driven navigation, clear hours, and private dining event promotion.

**What stands out:** Warm tones and inviting imagery make visitors feel welcome before they even walk through the door.

### 13\. [Katsuya](https://katsuyarestaurant.com/)

**Built with**: **WordPress**

![katsuya website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/katsuyarestaurant-com.jpg)

Contemporary Japanese cuisine by Master Sushi Chef Katsuya Uechi with Philippe Starck-designed interiors. Inventor of spicy tuna crispy rice..

Sleek WordPress site with Starck-influenced design aesthetic, global location showcase, and event catering integration.

**What stands out:** The modern, sleek design feels current and forward-thinking — exactly what clients look for.

### 14\. [Sushi Izu](https://sushiizu.com.au/)

**Built with**: **WordPress**

![sushi izu website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/sushiizu-com-au.jpg)

Australia’s largest sushi retailer. Hybrid Japanese meals made fresh daily with multiple locations nationwide..

Functional WordPress site with a custom theme focused on location finder and daily fresh meal messaging.

**What stands out:** A well-designed website that showcases Sushi Izu’s unique brand identity and makes a strong first impression.

### 15\. [The Omakase Room at Sushi-san](https://theomakaseroom.com/)

**Built with**: **WordPress**

![the omakase room at sushi-san website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/theomakaseroom-com.jpg)

Ten-seat, 18-course omakase by Master Sushi Chef Kaze Chan. Daily Toyosu Fish Market deliveries.

One of Chicago’s largest Japanese whisky and sake programs.. Intimate WordPress design conveying exclusivity, with dark palette and emphasis on the chef’s craft and sourcing story.

**What stands out:** The dark, atmospheric design creates a bold visual identity that perfectly suits The Omakase Room at Sushi-san’s brand.

### 16\. [Sushi by Bou](https://sushibybou.com/)

**Built with**: **Other**

![sushi by bou website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/sushibybou-com.jpg)

Intimate omakase hideaway with 12-course Signature, 17-course Bougie, and Bou Reserve experiences. Each location has unique themed design (Art Deco, Tokyo minimalist, Disco, Hip-Hop).

\*\*Platform: BentoBox\*\*. Immersive BentoBox design with location-specific themes, bold visual identity, and tiered experience presentation.

**What stands out:** The clean, minimalist layout lets the work speak for itself — no distractions, just great design.

### 17\. [Nobu](https://noburestaurants.com/)

**Built with**: **Other**

![nobu website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/noburestaurants-com.jpg)

Luxury fine-dining chain by Chef Nobuyuki Matsuhisa. Innovative Japanese-Peruvian fusion cuisine. Restaurants and hotels across five continents.

\*\*Platform: Custom (by PASTPRESENTFUTURE agency)\*\*. Custom-built luxury site with scroll-triggered animations, regional navigation, and bespoke interactive components befitting a global brand.

**What stands out:** The premium design aesthetic communicates luxury and exclusivity at every touchpoint.

### 18\. [Sushi Zo](https://sushizo.us/)

**Built with**: **Other**

![sushi zo website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/sushizo-us.jpg)

Multi-country fine-dining omakase concept. Traditional Edomae-style sushi across five countries.

\*\*Platform: Custom (jQuery/HTML)\*\*. Understated custom site with fullscreen background images, minimal navigation, and global location showcase.

**What stands out:** The clean, minimalist layout lets the work speak for itself — no distractions, just great design.

### 19\. [Omakase Sushi Dairo](https://omakasesushidairo.com/)

**Built with**: **Other**

![omakase sushi dairo website example](https://colorlib.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/omakasesushidairo-com.jpg)

Chef’s selection omakase with 11 pieces of sushi, sashimi, and seasonal courses ($160). Uses a blend of three Koshihikari rice varieties and two red vinegars.

\*\*Platform: WordPress\*\*. (Moved to WordPress section above if needed; currently WordPress with WooCommerce.) Story-driven WordPress design emphasizing rice and ingredient craft.

**What stands out:** Compelling visual storytelling draws visitors into the brand narrative from the first scroll.
