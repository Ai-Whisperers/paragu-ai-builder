### Not Your Typical Sushi Joint...

Nomzilla! sushi et cetera, is not just a sushi restaurant or even an asian fusion restaurant. That’s why “et cetera” is spelled out to emphasize that we do way more than just sushi. We challenge the traditional culinary norms by mixing the flavors of different cultures, so our customers enjoy the optimal nomlicious experience.

Located in the vibrant East Nashville community, we offer an ideal setting for customers with mild to adventurous palettes.
