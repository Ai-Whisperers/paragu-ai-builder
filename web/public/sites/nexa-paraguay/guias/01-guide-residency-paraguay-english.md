## 1. The Three Paths to Paraguayan Residency

### Options Comparison

| Feature | Temporary Residency | Permanent Residency | SUACE Investor |
|:--------|:---------------:|:----------------:|:------------:|
| **Initial duration** | 2 years | 10 years | Permanent |
| **Renewable** | Yes (2x up to 6 years) | Yes (10 years) | No |
| **Minimum investment** | None | None | USD 70,000 |
| **Processing time** | 45-60 days | 30-45 days | 45-90 days |
| **Path to citizenship** | 3 years after permanent | 3 years | 3 years |
| **Work permit** | ✓ Included | ✓ Included | ✓ Included |

:::{.callout-note}
**Key difference:** Temporary residency requires 21 months before converting to permanent. The SUACE path skips that period and gives you permanent residency from day one.
:::

---

### 1.1 Temporary Residency (Radicación Temporal)

**Who is it for?** Professionals, entrepreneurs, and families seeking to establish without minimum investment.

**Characteristics:**

- Validity: 2 years (renewable up to 6 years total)
- Work permit included
- You can register and operate a business
- Access to public healthcare
- After 21 months: conversion to permanent

**Costs:**

| Item | USD |
|:-----|---:|
| Government fees | ~$150 |
| Legal/professional services | $500-1,000 |
| **Total** | **$650-1,150** |

---

### 1.2 Permanent Residency (Radicación Permanente)

**Who is it for?** Those with 21+ months of temporary residency or seeking long-term establishment.

**Characteristics:**

- Validity: 10 years (renewable)
- Full rights: live, work, study
- Access to social services
- Path to citizenship after 3 years
- No minimum physical presence required

**Costs:**

| Item | USD |
|:-----|---:|
| Government fees | ~$150 |
| Legal/professional services | $500-1,000 |
| **Total** | **$650-1,150** |

---

### 1.3 SUACE Investor (USD 70,000+)

**Who is it for?** Investors seeking immediate permanent residency without the 21-month waiting period.

**Characteristics:**

- Minimum investment: USD 70,000 declared
- Direct permanent residency
- Accelerated process: 45-90 days
- Requires Paraguayan company
- Path to citizenship after 3 years

**Advantages:**

| Aspect | Temporary → Permanent | SUACE Investor |
|:-------|:------------------:|:-------------:|
| Time to permanent | 21 months | 45-90 days |
| Initial residency | Temporary | Permanent |
| Work authorization | After 21 months | Immediate |
| Total cost | $650-1,150 + time | $1,650-3,150 |

## 2. Document Requirements

### Complete document list

| # | Document | Requirement | Legalization |
|:--|:---------|:-----------|:-----------|
| 1 | Valid passport | Minimum 6 months validity | — |
| 2 | Birth certificate | Full copy | Apostille (Hague) |
| 3 | Criminal background check | From country of origin | Apostille |
| 4 | Recent photos | 4 copies, white background | — |
| 5 | Proof of financial means | Bank statement or contract | Notarized if foreign |

### Requirements by nationality

| Group | Legalization requirement |
|:-----|:----------------------|
| **EU/Schengen** | Apostille required |
| **United States** | Apostille required |
| **Mercosur** | Simple legalization (consular seal) |
| **Other countries** | Apostille required |

### Translation

- All documents in languages other than Spanish must be translated
- Public translator (registered with Supreme Court)
- Non-official translations not accepted

## 3. Detailed Timeline: Temporary Residency

### Week by Week

```
Week 1-2: Document Preparation
│
├── ■ Obtain apostille in home country
├── ■ Translate to Spanish (if applicable)
├── ■ Gather proof of financial means
└── □ Verify passport validity

│

├── ■ Data verification
└── □ Wait for approval decision

│
├── ■ Receive approval notification
├── ■ Schedule fingerprint appointment
├── ■ Receive temporary residency card

│
├── ■ Apply for Cédula de Identidad
├── ■ Biometric data collection (fingerprints, photo)

```

**Total: 8-12 weeks from submission → Cédula in hand**

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I work in Paraguay with temporary residency?

:::{.callout-success}
**Yes.** Temporary residency includes work permit. You can be employed or operate a business as a freelancer or company.
:::

### How long must I stay in Paraguay?

:::{.callout-success}
**No minimum physical presence required.** This is a significant advantage compared to Portugal (183 days/year) and Spain.
:::

### Can I have dual residency?

Paraguay does not prohibit dual residency. Tax residency rules apply separately. Consult a tax advisor about your home country's rules.

### Do I lose residency if I leave the country?

:::{.callout-success}
**No.** Paraguayan law does not establish minimum physical presence. You only lose residency by abandonment (no return for 3+ years) or criminal conviction.
:::

### Do I need a lawyer for the process?

Not required by law, but **very recommended.** The process involves:

- Documents in Spanish
- Legalizations (apostille)
- Specific institutional procedures
- Administrative forms

Most expatriates use a lawyer or professional service to ensure accuracy and speed.

### Can I operate a business with temporary residency?

:::{.callout-success}
**Yes.** You can register a company (EAS, SRL, SA) and operate fully with temporary residency. No restrictions.
:::

### How long is the Cédula valid?

The residency card is valid for 10 years. The Cédula (Paraguayan identity document) also requires renewal every 10 years.
