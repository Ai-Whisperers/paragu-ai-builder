### Key Differentiators

#### 1. "Territorial" vs "Worldwide" — What It Means

**Your current system (NL/BE/DE):**
- You pay tax on income wherever it's generated
- Even income earned outside your home country gets taxed at your home country rates
- Foreign investments, dividends, rental income — all taxed

**Paraguay's territorial system:**
- Only income *generated within Paraguay* is taxed
- Foreign income (dividends, royalties, consulting fees, rental income) = 0% tax
- This is legal and common in LATAM; Paraguay isn't on the EU blacklist

**How We Help:**
1. Coordinate with Dutch tax counsel before departure
2. Structure exit to minimize immediate exit tax
3. Time the exit to avoid double-taxation years
4. Document everything for Dutch tax authority

**The Key Insight:**
Exit tax isn't avoided — it's deferred. If you're leaving NL for Paraguay, the tax authority expects you to pay eventually. The question is: can you defer it long enough that the present value is significantly lower? Answer: often yes.
