## Differentiators (P2 — the things that win deals)

| Feature | Peers with it | Why it matters for Stoic Finch |
|---|---|---|
| Interactive **data maturity assessment** | 1-2 / 7 (rare, but huge conversion lift) | Captures intent + qualifies leads; 7-10% conversion in pro services per Pointerpro. |
| **ROI calculator** ("how much does a half-FTE save?") | 0 / 7 in this cohort, but standard SaaS pattern | Quantifies "Brag a Little" pillar concretely. |
| **Audit / health-check** lead magnet (free 1-hour review) | 1 / 7 (Seattle Data Guy → "Data Infrastructure Audit") | Low-friction first engagement; matches "No commitments" voice. |
| **Sample dashboard** / interactive demo | 2 / 7 (Stratus, Fuzeium teaser) | Shows craft; data-firm credibility. |
| **Service-tier pricing matrix** (Consultation / Development / Maintenance) | 0 / 7 (intentionally hidden by all) | Stoic Finch already has 3 engagement modes — could lead with a "starting from" range. |
| **Blog with technical depth** (real engineering posts) | 2 / 7 (Seattle Data Guy is the model) | Drives organic traffic; positions as experts. |
| **Podcast / YouTube / video** | 1 / 7 (Seattle Data Guy) | Personality-led marketing for boutique. |
| **Case study with quantified impact** (numbers + chart) | 3 / 7 | "Cut report time from 2hr to 5min" beats "we delivered a dashboard". |
| **Industry vertical landing pages** with sector copy | 4 / 7 | E.g. `/oil-and-gas-data-consulting-calgary` — local + vertical SEO gold. |
| **Founder story / "About the founder"** | 3 / 7 (Stratus, Seattle Data Guy) | Trust play for boutique buyers. |
| **Trust line: years in business / projects shipped** | 4 / 7 ("14 years", "200+ companies") | Stoic Finch has none publicly. |
| **Live chat / chatbot on contact** | 0 / 7 in this cohort | Low priority for B2B. Skip. |
| **Multi-language site** | 0 / 7 (all English) | Skip — Stoic Finch is EN-only by design. |
| **Localized landing pages** (e.g. "Power BI consultant Calgary") | 1 / 7 (ThinkTech "Odoo Calgary" / "Edmonton") | Geo-SEO play. High value if targeting Calgary mid-market. |

## Recommended phased roadmap

### **P0 — Trust gaps to close before any paid traffic**
*(All blockers come from data missing on the live site. No engineering.)*

1. Replace `placeholderFields` (in `site.json`) with real data:
   - `contact.email` — real intake address
   - `contact.phone` — Calgary area-code line
   - `location.address` — full street address
   - `location.coordinates` — for Google Maps embed (later)
2. Add 3-5 named testimonials (`testimonials-section`) with role + company + photo
3. Add 6-12 client logos (`trust-signals-logos-section`)
4. Add team page with founder bio + photo (`team-section`)
5. Flip `is_demo: false` and `isLiveProduction: true`

**Effort:** content gathering only. **Impact:** brings Stoic Finch from "AI-mirror demo" to "real boutique site."

### **P1 — Match table-stakes within 2 weeks**

6. Per-service pages (5 new pages) — `pages/services/{reports,analysis,science,engineering,strategy}.json` each with hero, deliverables, sample outcomes, mini-CTA
7. Case studies index page (`pages/case-studies.json`) + 3 case study pages (each with: client, problem, approach, stack, quantified result, quote)
8. Methodology page (`pages/methodology.json` or in-page anchor) — formalize "Consultation → Development → Maintenance" with deliverables per stage
9. Blog scaffolding (`pages/blog.json`) + 3 launch posts (founder authority): "Building your first dashboard", "When to hire vs outsource your data team", "5 signs you need a data engineer"
10. Newsletter signup (`newsletter-signup-section`) on home + blog
11. Privacy + Terms + Cookie pages (legal scaffolding) — `pages/legal/{privacy,terms,cookies}.json`
12. Multi-column footer with full site map (currently flat)
13. Add Microsoft + AWS / Databricks partner badges (if certified) — `trust-signals` variant
14. Stat trust line: "X years embedded · Y dashboards shipped · Z FTE-equivalent saved"

**Effort:** content + ~12 page configs. **No new code.**

### **P2 — Differentiators that drive qualified leads**

15. **Free Data Health Check** lead magnet — embed `intake-wizard-section` as a 5-question scoping form; instant LinkedIn Calendly invite. (Replaces the generic contact form for high-intent leads.)
16. **Calendly booking** (`booking-embed-section`) on contact + as a sticky button
17. **Industry vertical landing pages** — `pages/industries/{oil-and-gas,manufacturing,healthcare,financial-services,saas}.json` (5 pages, 1500-2000 words each, identical layout, vertical-specific copy + case study cards)
18. **Local-SEO landing pages** — `pages/locations/{calgary,edmonton,vancouver}.json` ("Data consulting in Calgary"); each ranks for "{service} {city}"
19. Quantified case studies (3 per vertical, 15 total over time)
20. Founder story page (`our-story-section`) — Calgary origin, what "stoic" means to the team
21. Service-tier pricing matrix (`compare-plans-matrix-section`) — even with "starting from" ranges; transparency builds trust
22. Categorized FAQ (`faq-categorized-section`) with 12-20 questions

**Effort:** ~10 new pages + 1 lead-magnet wiring. **No new code.**

### **P3 — Long-tail growth & moats**

23. **Data maturity self-assessment** with scoring + result tier — *new component required* (`maturity-assessment-section.tsx`). Highest-converting lead magnet for pro services (7-10% per industry data).
24. **Interactive sample dashboard** — embed a public Power BI dashboard demonstrating actual craft
25. **Resources hub** — glossary, downloadable templates ("Dashboard QA checklist.pdf"), success-story PDFs, with email gating
26. **Podcast / YouTube** — short founder explainers (Seattle Data Guy model)
27. **Customer-portal area** (login-gated) — for active engagements; deferred until book-of-business justifies it
28. Localized landing pages for cities Stoic Finch wants to win in next 12 months
29. Multi-language? (skip — confirmed EN-only)

## Acceptance criteria for top 5 P0/P1 items

### #1 Real testimonials (P0)
- ≥3 testimonials in `content/en.json` under `home.testimonialsList`
- Each: quote (max 280 chars), name, role, company, optional photo path under `sites/stoicfinch/images/testimonials/`
- Add `testimonials-section` to `pages/home.json` between `services` and `process-timeline`
- Add `testimonials: true` to `site.json.features`

### #2 Per-service pages (P1)
- `pages/services/reports-dashboarding.json` (and 4 siblings)
- Each has: header, hero, features (3 deliverables), services (sub-services if any), case-studies teaser, contact CTA, footer
- Update `static-sites.ts` `pages` array for `stoicfinch` to list all 5 new slugs
- All 5 link from `home.json` services cards via `ctaHref`

### #3 Case studies (P1)
- `pages/case-studies.json` index page + 3 detail pages (`pages/case-studies/{slug}.json`)
- Each detail page schema: client logo, situation, intervention, stack used, result with metric, named quote
- New content key: `caseStudies` keyed by slug

### #4 Free Data Health Check (P2)
- New page: `pages/data-health-check.json` using `intake-wizard-section`
- 5-step form (~5 min): industry → data sources → top pain → maturity → email
- Submit posts to existing lead-form API; routes to Calendly thank-you
- Replace primary hero CTA from "Get Started Today" to "Get a Free Data Health Check"

### #5 Industry vertical pages (P2)
- 5 pages, identical schema, sector-specific copy:
  - `pages/industries/oil-and-gas.json` (Calgary-relevant)
  - `pages/industries/manufacturing.json`
  - `pages/industries/healthcare.json`
  - `pages/industries/financial-services.json`
  - `pages/industries/b2b-saas.json`
- Each: hero, 3-pillar benefits scoped to industry, 3 services scoped to industry, 1-2 case-study teasers, contact
- Add to footer "Industries" column

## Effort estimate

| Phase | Pages added | Components needed | Content effort | Eng effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | 0 | 0 (data only) | 1-2 days (testimonials, photos, copy) | 0.5 day (wire `testimonials-section` + flip flags) |
| P1 | 12 (5 services + 3 case studies + blog + 3 legal) | 0 | 1-2 weeks (case study research, blog writing) | 2-3 days (page configs) |
| P2 | 10 (5 industries + 3 locations + lead magnet + Calendly) | 0 | 2 weeks | 3-4 days |
