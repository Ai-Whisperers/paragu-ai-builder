# Nexa Paraguay — Sales Call Scripts
**Updated:** April 2026

> **Purpose:** Live conversation guides for sales calls. Different from objection handling (text/email) — this is for real-time conversations.

---

## 1. Call Opening (First 90 Seconds)

### Script

```
"[Hola/Hi]! Thanks for booking the call. This is [Name] from Nexa Paraguay.

I've got 30 minutes scheduled with you — we'll make the most of it.

Before we dive in, quick context: I help people relocate to Paraguay with a focus on tax optimization. Our sweet spot is people earning €60K+ who are tired of paying 40-50% in taxes.

What's top of mind for you today? What brought you to look at Paraguay?"
```

### Purpose

| Goal | Why |
|------|-----|
| Build rapport | First impression sets tone |
| Qualify intent | "What brought you here?" → their pain |
| Time-box | 30 min = finite, respect their time |
| Set context | You work with their profile |

### Response Handling

| If they say... | Pivot to... |
|--------------|-----------||
| "Just browsing" | "That's fair. What specifically caught your eye?" |
| "My friend recommended" | "Great — what did they tell you?" |
| "I have questions" | "Perfect — we'll get to all of them" |
| "I'm serious about moving" | "Let's talk numbers" |

## 2. Discovery (5 Minutes)

### Qualification Framework

```
Quick 5 questions to understand fit:

1. "What's your current country?" (NL/BE/DE = hot)
2. "What's your annual income roughly?" (€60K+ = hot)
3. "Is this for you or a company?" (mixed = interesting)
4. "What's your timeline?" (now/3mo = hot; "sometime" = nurture)
5. "What's the main driver?" (tax = HOT; "residency" = warm)
```

### Note Talking Points

| Data Point | What It Tells You |
|----------|-----------------|
| Current country | Email + tax jurisdiction |
| Income level | Deal size, savings potential |
| Timeline | Urgency, close readiness |
| Driver | Marketing message alignment |

### Discovery Questions (Natural Flow)

```
[After opening]

"Cool, I want to make sure this call is worth your time. A few quick questions:

What's your current situation? Where are you based now, and what are you currently doing for work?"

[After they answer]

"What's the main thing prompting the move? Tax situation, lifestyle, business — what's driving this?"

[After they answer]

"What's your timeline looking like? Are we talking soon, this year, or just exploring?"

[After they answer]

"Perfect. One more — what's the income picture? I ask because our sweet spot is people earning €60K+ who could save €25-40K/year. That align with you?"
```

## 3. Value Proposition (5 Minutes)

### When They've Answered "Tax"

```
"That's actually our specialty. Here's the math:

In [NL/BE/DE], you're looking at 37-50% in taxes. In Paraguay, it's 10% flat (IRE) on income generated in Paraguay, and foreign income? Zero. Territorial.

For a €100K income, that's roughly €30-40K in savings per year.

The residency is how we get there — the tax optimization is the real value.

What's your income situation look like? Want me to run your specific numbers?"
```

### When They've Answered "Residency"

```
"Residency is part of it, but here's what most people miss: the 10% IRE + territorial system is what makes this worth doing. Residency is just the mechanism.

Portugal's old NHR is dead. Spain requires €500K investment + 183 days/year. Paraguay: zero investment, zero days after you get residency.

Is tax optimization part of your thinking at all, or is it purely residency?"
```

### Timeline Response

| Timeline | Response |
|----------|---------|
| "Now" | "Let's move" — high urgency |
| "3 months" | "We can work with that" |
| "This year" | "Lock in timeline" |
| "Exploring" | Educational call, soft close |

## 4. Handling Objections (Live)

### "I'm still thinking"

```
"That's completely fair. This is a big decision.

Quick question: what's the one thing you're thinking through? Is it the location, the money, getting spouse buy-in, or something else?"
```

**[If they say money]:**
"Let me reframe: what's expensive is paying 40% when you could pay 10%. Our fee pays for itself in 3-4 months of tax savings. Want me to show you?"

**[If they say location]:**
"Have you visited? Because we can arrange a scouting trip — it's very different from what the news makes it look."

**[If they say spouse]:**
"Bring them on a call. We'd love to address both your concerns. No pressure — we can do a second call."

### "It's too expensive"

```
"I get it upfront. But here's the question: what's the cost of not doing it?

If you're paying 40% and could be paying 10%, that's €30-40K/year. Our fee is one-time. How quickly does that pay for itself?

Also — we have payment plans if that helps. But the bigger question is: is the number the issue, or is it the uncertainty about whether it's right for you?"
```

### "What about banking?"

```
"That's the #1 question we get. Here's the real talk: banking in Paraguay is hard — that's why most DIY attempts fail.

We have relationships with Banco do Brasil, Banco Continental, Itau. We introduce you, provide documentation, and in most cases, you walk out with an account during your 2-day trip.

It's not guaranteed (the bank decides), but our success rate is high because we know exactly what they want.

Did you try before? Or is this your first go?"
```

### "My partner isn't on board"

```
"Bring them on a call! No seriously — often it's the partner who's skeptical who becomes the biggest fan once they see the numbers.

We can do 30 minutes with both of you, address their specific concerns. What does your partner seem most worried about?"
```

### "I need to speak with my accountant/lawyer"

```
"Absolutely — and we want that. We don't replace your home-country advisor; we coordinate with them. Our team has worked with most major NL/BE tax firms.

We can jump on a call with your accountant — let them ask whatever they want. No surprises.

Do you want us to reach out to them, or do you prefer to bring them in?"
```

## 5. Closing Techniques (Last 5 Minutes)

### Call to Action - When Ready

```
"Alright — here's where we are:

You fit our profile, the numbers make sense. If you're ready to move forward:

1. I send over the service agreement (takes 24 hours to draft)
2. You review — any questions, we clarify
3. We sign, you pay deposit, we file

We're running about 2-3 weeks out before filing. Does that timeline work for you?

If you're not ready — that's cool too. But from what you've told me, you're looking at €30-40K/year in savings. That doesn't get better by waiting."
```

### Call to Action - Soft Close

```
"Look — you're clearly thinking about this seriously. From my side, the question is simple:

Are you in, or are you in?

If it's yes, let's move. If it's no, tell me. Either way, I'll be direct with you.

What's your gut telling you?"
```

### Call to Action - Calendar Lock

```
"Let me ask: if we do this, when would you want to start?

Because we can file as soon as you give us the green light. No pressure — but does [date in 2-3 weeks] work for a target?"
```

### After They Say "Let me think"

```
"I hear you. Here's what I'd ask: what's the deciding factor? Is there something I haven't answered, or is it just the gravity of the decision?

Because from what we've discussed, the math adds up.

What I can offer: take the weekend, crunch the numbers. But let's not leave this hanging. Can we do a follow-up call [Date] to make a call?"
```

- [ ] Review lead source (where did they come from?)

- [ ] Note their country, income level, timeline from booking

- [ ] Have Calendly link ready for follow-up

### During Call

- [ ] First 90 seconds: rapport + context
- [ ] Discovery: 5 questions, take notes
- [ ] Value prop: tie to their specific situation
- [ ] Objections: address in real-time
- [ ] Close: specific next step

### After Call

- [ ] Send summary email same day
- [ ] Update CRM with notes
- [ ] Tag lead with outcome
- [ ] Schedule follow-up if needed
- [ ] If booked: trigger service agreement
